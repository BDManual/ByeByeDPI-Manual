If the official YouTube application does not meet your needs, consider one of these alternatives.

#### TVs and set-top boxes

- [YouTube for Android TV](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.youtube.tv) — the official remote-friendly YouTube application. Requires Android 7.0 or newer.
- [SmartTube](https://github.com/yuliskov/SmartTube) — a client with built-in proxy support. Requires Android 4.3 or newer.
- [TizenTube Cobalt](https://github.com/reisxd/TizenTubeCobalt) — removes ads and sponsored segments. Requires Android 7.0 or newer.

#### Patched YouTube

- YouTube ReVanced. [ReVanced Manager](https://revanced.app/download) simplifies downloading and updating ReVanced and MicroG. [revanced.app](https://revanced.app/) is the official website. Requires Android 8.0 or newer.
- [Morphe](https://morphe.software/) — an alternative patch project from developers who previously created YouTube and YouTube Music modifications. Requires Android 9.0 or newer.

> [!TIP]
> It is recommended to patch ReVanced and Morphe yourself:

- [YouTube ReVanced Manager](https://github.com/ReVanced/revanced-manager)
- [Morphe Manager](https://github.com/MorpheApp/morphe-manager)

#### NewPipe and its variants

- [NewPipe](https://github.com/TeamNewPipe/NewPipe/releases) — a YouTube client that does not depend on Google libraries. Google account sign-in is not available. Requires Android 5.0 or newer.
- [BravePipe](https://github.com/bravenewpipe/NewPipe) — a NewPipe fork with SponsorBlock, improved search and sorting, and Google Takeout subscription import. Android 4.4 support is unverified.
- [PipePipe](https://github.com/InfinityLoop1308/PipePipe) — a NewPipe fork with additional services, client features, and fixes. Requires Android 5.0 or newer.
- [Tubular](https://github.com/polymorphicshade/Tubular) — a NewPipe fork with SponsorBlock and Return YouTube Dislike. Requires Android 5.0 or newer.

#### SkyTube

- [SkyTube Extra](https://github.com/SkyTubeTeam/SkyTube) — a lightweight YouTube player with support for the official player and Chromecast. Requires Android 4.4 or newer.
- [SkyTube](https://github.com/SkyTubeTeam/SkyTube) — a free YouTube client that does not require Google Play Services. Requires Android 4.4 or newer.
- [SkyTube Legacy Extra](https://github.com/SkyTubeTeam/SkyTubeLegacy) — the legacy Extra version for Android 4.0 or newer.
- [SkyTube Legacy](https://github.com/SkyTubeTeam/SkyTubeLegacy) — the legacy free client for Android 4.0 or newer.

#### Privacy-focused clients

> [!WARNING]
> These applications depend on third-party instances. The instances may be blocked differently from YouTube and may need to be [unblocked separately](/en/docs/strategies/#unlock-more-services).

- [FreeTube Android](https://github.com/MarmadileManteater/FreeTubeAndroid) — uses the Invidious API. Requires Android 7.0 or newer.
- [LibreTube](https://github.com/libre-tube/LibreTube) — uses the [Piped API](https://github.com/TeamPiped/Piped), does not require a YouTube account or Google services.
- [Mini YouTube](https://github.com/monobogdan/selfeco) — an Invidious-based client from SelfEco for Android 2.2 or newer. An unverified [fork](https://github.com/tinelix/selfeco-plus) claims HTTP proxy support but must be built manually.
- [notPipe](https://github.com/gohoski/notPipe) — a client for Android 1.5 or newer that uses [Invidious](https://invidious.io), [yt2009](https://github.com/ftde0/yt2009), and [YtAPILegacy](http://yt.modyleprojects.ru).

#### More options

- [VueTube](https://github.com/pixkk/VueTube) — a Vue-based client with SponsorBlock and Return YouTube Dislike. Requires Android 5.0 or newer and may not support Android 12.1 or newer.
- [Namida](https://github.com/namidaco/namida) — a music and video player with YouTube support.
- [YT PRO](https://github.com/prateek-chaubey/YTPro) — a lightweight wrapper for `m.youtube.com`. Requires Android 5.0 or newer.
- [NouTube](https://github.com/nonbili/NouTube) — combines YouTube and YouTube Music, supports background playback, and removes ads.
- [pstube](https://github.com/prateekmedia/pstube) — watches and downloads videos without ads.

#### Proprietary applications

- [PureTuber](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=free.tube.premium.advanced.tuber) — a NewPipe-based client with a different interface and Google account sign-in. Requires Android 4.4 or newer.
- [GreenTuber](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=by.green.tuber&hl=en) — a YouTube client with ad blocking. Requires Android 6.0 or newer.
